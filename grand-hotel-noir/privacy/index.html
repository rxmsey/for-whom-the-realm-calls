Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 September 2026 · Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grand Hotel Noir: Mystery Tycoon handles information when you use the iOS app.

Short version:

The current version does not use advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, developer-operated analytics, or a developer-operated account system. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores information required to run the game, including hotel progress, room and staff upgrades, mystery progress, settings, virtual currency balances, purchase-fulfilment records and other gameplay state. This information is stored locally in the app's iOS storage.

2. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Optional purchases, including Gem packs, premium items and Grand Hotel Club, are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Apple handles payment information. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

To fulfil purchases, restore eligible entitlements and prevent duplicate fulfilment, the app may process StoreKit information such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, subscription status and transaction verification status. Relevant fulfilment records may be stored locally on your device.

3. Data the app does not intentionally collect

The current version does not intentionally collect or request:

  • your name or a developer-created account;
  • precise or coarse location;
  • contacts;
  • photos or photo-library contents;
  • camera or microphone data;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • cross-app or cross-website tracking data; or
  • data through third-party advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

4. Third-party services

Apple / App Store / StoreKit

Purchases, subscriptions and App Store services are provided by Apple. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

GitHub Pages

This privacy, terms and support website is hosted with GitHub Pages. The site contains no advertising or analytics scripts added by the developer. GitHub may process ordinary website request information as the hosting provider under GitHub's own privacy terms.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is replaced, reset, or removed with the app's local data. Deleting the app normally removes its local app data, subject to device-level backup or restore features controlled by you and Apple.

If you voluntarily contact support, the information you choose to send may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate your request, provide support, prevent abuse or fraud, or meet applicable legal obligations.

6. Your choices

  • You can choose not to make optional in-app purchases or subscribe to Grand Hotel Club.
  • You can manage or cancel App Store subscriptions through your Apple account settings.
  • You can reset or remove locally stored game data using available in-app or iOS controls.
  • You can contact support to ask about information you voluntarily submitted to the developer.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not provide a developer-operated account system and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you believe personal information was voluntarily sent to support by a child, contact the developer so it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
