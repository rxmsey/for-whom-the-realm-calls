In-app purchases and Gems

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. If Apple shows a purchase as completed but the purchased content does not appear, close and reopen the game while connected to the internet, then use Restore Purchases where applicable. If the issue remains, contact support with the approximate purchase time and any Apple transaction information available to you. Do not send payment-card details.

Refund requests for App Store purchases are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Grand Hotel Club

Grand Hotel Club is an optional auto-renewable monthly subscription. The current in-app purchase screen shows the localized price, one-month billing period and advertised member benefits before purchase. You can manage or cancel the subscription through your Apple account subscription settings. If the subscription expires, recurring member benefits stop at the end of the paid period.

See the Terms of Use for the full subscription terms.

Save data and progress

The current version stores hotel progress locally on your device and does not use a developer-operated account or cloud-save server. Deleting the app can remove local progress. Device-level backups and restores are controlled by iOS and Apple.

Gameplay or mystery issues

If a room, guest, staff member or mystery appears stuck, first close and reopen the game. If the issue returns, include the room number or mystery name and what you were doing immediately before the problem occurred when you contact support.
